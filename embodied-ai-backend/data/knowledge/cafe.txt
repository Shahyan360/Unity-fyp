A good café greeting is brief and welcoming. Open body language, eye contact when culturally comfortable, and an easy question can invite conversation without putting someone on the spot.
For a polite café order, greet the barista, name the item and any preference, ask for the total if needed, and finish with a thank-you. In this Unity experience orders are simulated; no money is charged.
Small talk can start with a shared, low-pressure topic such as a drink, the music, or a book. Follow the other person's interest and offer space if they seem busy.
Supportive conversation practice focuses on the speaker's intention, one observable strength, and one useful next step. It avoids judging someone's accent, identity, or personality.
If a café is busy, concise and patient ordering helps. If you need a modification, state it clearly and politely, and check whether it is possible rather than assuming.
When a person gives a short answer, try a gentle open question, share a little about yourself, or let the conversation pause naturally.
